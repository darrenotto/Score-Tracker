Tennis rules: how to score and play

A short, casual-play summary of tennis scoring and rules. Leagues and clubs vary, so check your local rules.

How to score

  • Points in a game go 0, 15, 30, 40, then game.
  • At 40–40 (deuce) you need two points in a row (advantage, then game).
  • Usually first to 6 games wins a set, by 2 games (the app lets you pick 4 to 8).
  • At 6–6 a tiebreak is played: first to 7 points, by 2, wins the set 7–6.

Service

  • Serve from behind the baseline, diagonally into the service box.
  • You get two attempts; missing both is a double fault.
  • Serve alternates each game.

In & out

  • A ball touching the line is in. Beyond the lines is out.

Winning

  • Matches are usually best of 3 sets — use Tournament mode.

Scoring tennis in DuelPoin

Tap the side that wins each POINT. The app tracks 15/30/40, deuce, games and the tiebreak. At setup you can set your house rules: games per set (4 to 8, usually 6) and the deuce rule (standard deuce with advantage, or golden point: one deciding point at 40–40). Quick Match is one set; use Tournament and "Best of 3 Sets" for a full match. Optional: "Track who's serving" shows the server and which side they serve from.
