Sport rules

Short, plain-English rules for the sports DuelPoin keeps score for. Leagues and clubs vary, so check your local rules.

  • Badminton rules: Tap to score to 21, win by 2 (max 30).
  • Volleyball rules: Tap to score to 25, win by 2.
  • Tennis rules: Points, games and tiebreaks, with standard deuce or golden point.
  • Table Tennis rules: Tap to score to 11, win by 2.
  • Padel rules: Points, games and tiebreaks, with golden point or advantage.
  • Basketball rules: Count points to any target you choose.
  • Squash rules: Tap to score to 11 or 15, win by 2.
