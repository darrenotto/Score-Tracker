Volleyball rules: how to score and play

A short, casual-play summary of volleyball scoring and rules. Leagues and clubs vary, so check your local rules.

How to score

  • Rally scoring: every rally is a point.
  • First to 25 wins the set, but you must win by 2 (no cap).
  • A deciding final set is often played to 15.

Service & rotation

  • Serve from behind the end line; the same player serves until their side loses a rally.
  • When a side wins the serve back, its players rotate one position clockwise.

Play

  • Each side gets at most 3 touches. The same player can't hit twice in a row (a block doesn't count).
  • The ball must cross the net; touching the net during play is a fault.

In & out

  • The line is in. Landing outside the lines is out.

Winning

  • Matches are best of 3 or 5 sets — use Tournament mode.

Scoring volleyball in DuelPoin

Each game is scored to 25, win by 2. The shorter 15-point deciding set is not automatic — use Custom/Basketball-style targets if you need it.
