Basketball rules: how to score and play

A short, casual-play summary of basketball scoring and rules. Leagues and clubs vary, so check your local rules.

How to score

  • 1 point for a free throw, 2 for a shot inside the arc, 3 for a shot beyond it.
  • Casual games are often "first to 11, 15 or 21", sometimes counting 1s and 2s only.

Play

  • Dribble to move; no carrying or double-dribbling.
  • The ball is out when it touches the boundary or goes beyond it — the other team gets it.

Winning

  • First to the agreed score. Many pick-up games win by 1; agree this before you start.

Scoring basketball in DuelPoin

You set the target score. Tap once per point, so tap twice for a two-pointer. There is no win-by-2.
