About DuelPoin

DuelPoin stops the arguments about the score. It is a free, fast score keeper for casual players: you and your friends on a badminton court, a padel court, around a table-tennis table or on a Sunday-morning tennis court.

Who it is for

Casual and club players who want a scoreboard they can trust without installing anything or creating an account. Open the site on one phone, tap a sport, and tap the side that wins each point. DuelPoin knows the scoring rules for Badminton, Volleyball, Tennis, Table Tennis, Padel, Basketball, Squash.

What it does

  • Tap, tap, done. One tap from the sport list to a live scoreboard.
  • No sign-up. Your matches stay on your own device.
  • Works offline. After your first visit the scoreboard keeps working with no signal, and a refresh or a closed browser never loses your score.
  • Undo any tap, rematch with a running series tally, and share the result as a picture.
  • Tennis and padel house rules: choose games per set and standard deuce or golden point.
  • Tournaments and drop-in court sessions when you need more than a single match.
