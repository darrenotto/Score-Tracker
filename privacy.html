Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

DuelPoin (duelpoin.com) is a free score keeper for casual games. This page describes what the site actually does with data.

Your matches stay on your device

Match scores, team and player names, match settings and any photos you take with the camera button are saved only in your own browser (localStorage) on the device you use. We never receive them, and there are no accounts. You can delete them at any time by deleting the match in the app or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Messages you send us

If you send a message through the contact form, or feedback from inside the app, what you type (and your email address, only if you enter one) is delivered to us by Formspree, a third-party form service. Formspree's handling of that data is described in its privacy policy. We use messages only to answer you and to improve DuelPoin.

Visit statistics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits to this site. It does not use cookies.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

DuelPoin may show ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more about how Google uses data when you use our site, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Privacy questions

For any privacy question, please use the contact page.
