Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

DuelPoin (duelpoin.com) is a free score keeper for casual sport. This page explains, in plain words, what the app stores, what it sends, and how advertising works.

What stays on your device

Your scores, team and player names, match settings and any photos you take are saved only in your own browser (localStorage) on the device you use. They are not uploaded to our servers, and there are no accounts. Photos are taken with your camera only when you press the camera button. You can remove everything at any time by deleting the match in the app or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Optional Google sign-in

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, Google tells the app your first name and profile picture address, which are kept only in your browser to say "Hi, name". We do not receive your email address. You can also use DuelPoin as a guest.

Sharing a result

When you press Share, the result image is created on your device and handed to your phone's share sheet (or downloaded). Nothing is sent to us. The link that goes with a share contains ?src=share so we can count how many visitors arrive through shared results.

Feedback you send

If you send feedback from inside the app, your rating, chosen topics, comment and (only if you type one) email address are sent to our form provider, Formspree, which emails them to us. We also include the sport you were on, your browser's user-agent text and the link or channel you arrived from. If you are offline, the message waits on your device and is sent when you are back online. We use feedback only to improve DuelPoin, and we only show a comment on the website if you ticked the box that allows it.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits to this site (which pages are viewed, in aggregate). It does not use cookies.

DuelPoin may use Google Analytics to count anonymous events such as "a match was started or finished", the sport, and which link or QR code brought you to the site. It never receives your team names, scores or photos. Google Analytics sets cookies or similar identifiers. You can opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

DuelPoin is free and may show advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, to measure them, and, where the law allows and you have not opted out, to personalise them based on your visits to this and other websites. Ads appear only on menu, setup and result screens. They never appear on the live scoring screen.

  • How Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's advertising technologies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Manage or turn off personalised ads: Google Ads Settings, or aboutads.info

Children

DuelPoin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this page

If we change how DuelPoin handles data, we will update this page and its date.
