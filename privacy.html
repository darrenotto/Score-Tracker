Privacy

DRAFT — review before publishing; this is not legal advice.

ScoreTracker stores your scores, team names and photos only in your own browser (localStorage) on this device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers, and there are no accounts.

Photos are taken with your device camera only when you press the camera button, and stay on your device until you delete them or clear the tournament.

Signing in with Google is optional. If you do, Google tells the app your first name and profile picture URL, which are kept only in this browser to say "Hi, name". We don't receive your email address and don't store anything on a server. You can also use the app as a guest.

When analytics is switched on, ScoreTracker uses Google Analytics to count anonymous events such as "a match was started or finished" together with the sport and the link or channel you arrived from (for example a QR code at a court).
